OUR STORY

Modest fashion for every beautiful occasion.

FatimaCollection is a modest fashion and tailoring brand based in Waterloo, Freetown, Sierra Leone.

We offer elegant abayas, open abayas, original cotton skirts, beautiful Muslim women's occasion dresses and stylish Muslim men's occasion outfits.

We also create custom modest designs for customers who want a personal style, a special outfit or a tailored piece made with care.

Whether you are looking for everyday modest clothing or something special for an occasion, we are here to help you find a style that feels right for you.
