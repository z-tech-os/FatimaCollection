HELP & FAQ

Questions? We are happy to help.

Find helpful answers about FatimaCollection modest fashion, tailoring, collections and ordering in Waterloo, Freetown.

What does FatimaCollection offer?

We offer elegant modest clothing including abayas, open abayas, original cotton skirts, beautiful Muslim women's occasion dresses and Muslim men's occasion outfits.

Do you offer custom modest designs?

Yes. We offer custom modest designs and tailoring. You can discuss your preferred style, fabric, colour, measurements and finishing details with us.

How can I place an order?

Send us a message on WhatsApp and tell us what you are interested in. We will discuss the item, design and other details with you.

Can I ask about prices before ordering?

Yes. You can contact us on WhatsApp about the item or design you are interested in, and we can discuss the current details with you.

Do you make both women's and men's occasion wear?

Yes. Our collections include beautiful Muslim women's occasion dresses as well as stylish Muslim men's occasion outfits for special events and celebrations.

Do you sell abayas and open abayas?

Yes. FatimaCollection offers elegant abayas and open abayas as part of our modest fashion collection.

Do you offer original cotton skirts?

Yes. Original cotton skirts are part of our modest fashion collection and can be explored along with our other clothing options.

Where are you located?

FatimaCollection is located at 18 White Stone Waterloo, Freetown, Sierra Leone.

Can I ask questions before ordering?

Absolutely. You are welcome to contact us with questions about our clothing, designs, tailoring service, availability or ordering process before making a decision.
