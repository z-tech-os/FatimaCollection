# FatimaCollection

## Elegant Modest Fashion & Tailoring

FatimaCollection is a modest fashion and tailoring brand based in **Waterloo, Freetown, Sierra Leone**.

The website presents FatimaCollection's clothing collections, tailoring services, style guidance, business information, FAQs, contact details, privacy policy and terms of service in a simple, elegant and mobile-friendly experience.

## 🌸 About FatimaCollection

FatimaCollection focuses on elegant and modest fashion for different occasions.

The business offers:

- Elegant modest clothing
- Abayas
- Open abayas
- Original cotton skirts
- Muslim women's occasion dresses
- Muslim men's occasion outfits

- Tailoring services

Customers can contact FatimaCollection to ask about available styles, prices, measurements, custom designs and orders.

## 🧵 Tailoring Process

FatimaCollection's custom tailoring service follows a simple process:

1. **Tell Us Your Idea**
   - Share the type of outfit or design you want.

2. **Choose Your Style**
   - Discuss the design, fabric, colour, fit and finishing.

3. **Provide Measurements**
   - Measurements are discussed according to the selected design.

4. **Made With Care**
   - The agreed design is prepared with attention to detail and finishing.
