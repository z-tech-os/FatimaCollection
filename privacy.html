PRIVACY

Your privacy matters to us.

We respect the information you share with FatimaCollection when you ask about our modest fashion, occasion wear or tailoring services.

Information We Receive

When you contact FatimaCollection, you may choose to provide information such as your name, phone number, measurements, preferred design, clothing requirements or order details.

How Information Is Used

Information you provide may be used to respond to enquiries, discuss available clothing, understand your tailoring requirements, prepare custom modest designs, communicate about orders and provide our services.

Information You Share Through WhatsApp

If you contact FatimaCollection through WhatsApp, the information you send, including messages, measurements, design preferences or order details, may be used to communicate with you and handle your enquiry or order. WhatsApp's own privacy policy may also apply to your use of its service.

Third-Party Services

This website uses services and links such as Google Fonts, Font Awesome, WhatsApp and the FatimaCollection social media pages. These services may have their own privacy policies and terms that apply when you use them.

Information Protection

We aim to handle information shared with FatimaCollection responsibly and only use it for legitimate communication, enquiries, orders and tailoring-related services. However, no online communication method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to our services or website. Any updated version will be published on this page.
